## Featured Projects

### ⚡ [London Electricity Demand Forecasting](https://github.com/Ghadah-Almaawy/london-electricity-demand-forecasting)
Day-ahead forecast of household electricity demand from **167.8 million** smart meter readings, processed with **PySpark**.
- Gradient Boosting reaches **3.3% MAPE**, 39% better than the best baseline, and stays stable across four test months
- Tests whether time-of-use pricing shifted evening demand with a difference-in-differences design and confidence intervals

`PySpark` `scikit-learn` `Time Series` `Causal Inference`

### 🏷️ [Coupon Campaign Revenue Prediction](https://github.com/Ghadah-Almaawy/coupon-campaign-revenue-prediction)
Predicts the revenue of promotional coupon campaigns from their setup and branch history.
- XGBoost explains **95.6%** of revenue variance and holds up on future campaigns
- Cleaned dates in four formats, currency text, and inconsistent labels in 20,400 campaigns

`XGBoost` `Regression` `Data Cleaning`

### 🩺 [Maternal Health Risk Prediction](https://github.com/Ghadah-Almaawy/maternal-health-risk-prediction)
Predicts pregnancy risk level (low / mid / high) from six vital signs.
- Found that **55% of records are duplicates**, inflating the usual score from 0.63 to 0.84; all results use grouped cross validation
- Every patient the final model flags as high risk is truly high risk (precision **1.00**)

`Classification` `Data Leakage` `Gradient Boosting`

### 👁️ [Eye Strain Prediction](https://github.com/Ghadah-Almaawy/Eye-Strain-prediction) · [Live App](https://ghadah-eye-strain.streamlit.app/)
Predicts digital eye strain from device usage habits, deployed as an interactive web app.
- **76.7%** accuracy against a 68.5% majority baseline, with no overfitting

`Logistic Regression` `Streamlit` `Deployment`

### 📈 [Stocks & Economic Indicators Dashboard](https://github.com/Ghadah-Almaawy/Stocks-Economic-Indicators-Dashboard-)
Four-page Power BI dashboard linking Saudi stock data with economic indicators.
- DAX-driven investment recommendations, GDP growth forecast, and a decomposition tree for root cause exploration

`Power BI` `DAX` `Data Modeling`
